Reduce Accounts Receivable

CurePay helps private practices collect more of what they earn by making patient payments simple and automatic. Our integrated platform supports text-to-pay, contactless checkout, recurring charges, ongoing treatment plans, and balance recovery for amounts not covered by insurance.

How this works in a private practice:

  • A dermatology office sends a text to pay link as soon as insurance adjudicates, so patients can settle the remaining balance from their phone instead of ignoring paper statements.
  • A physical therapy clinic sets up recurring charges for ongoing treatment plans, which eliminates manual billing and reduces missed payments each month.
  • A family medicine practice uses contactless checkout to collect copays at the time of service, which cuts down on open balances and shortens the billing cycle.

When patients have convenient ways to pay, accounts receivable shrinks and cash flow improves.

Streamline Operations

CurePay integrates directly with most EMRs and practice management software so payment posting happens automatically. This reduces administrative work, limits errors, and frees up your team to focus on patient care rather than billing tasks.

How this works in a private practice:

  • A pediatric office eliminates the need for staff to manually post credit card transactions, since CurePay syncs payments back into the EMR in real time.
  • A chiropractic clinic uses our integrated tools to manage payment plans for long-term treatment packages without keeping spreadsheets or paper logs.
  • A dental office protects the health of staff and patients by offering contactless payment.

CurePay's discreet payment process keeps financial conversations smooth and simple, protecting the doctor/patient relationship.

Secure and Compliant

CurePay is built for healthcare. Our technology is HIPAA compliant and PCI certified to make sure patient data stays protected at every stage of the payment process.

What this means for your practice:

  • Payment information is encrypted from end to end.
  • Sensitive data never touches your internal systems, lowering your risk exposure.
  • Patients feel more comfortable paying digitally because they know their information is secured to healthcare level standards.

Compliance and security are never an afterthought—they are built into the foundation of our platform.

Preserve and Enhance the Doctor/Patient Relationship

CurePay makes payments easier for patients and simpler for your staff. With all healthcare payments in one place, communication stays clear and predictable, which helps strengthen trust between your practice and your patients.

How this benefits your practice:

  • Patients appreciate simple payment requests sent by text instead of mailed statements.
  • Providers avoid uncomfortable collection conversations because the system handles reminders and follow-ups automatically.
  • Staff spends less time discussing balances and more time supporting care.

A better payment experience leads to happier patients and fewer administrative headaches.

Everything in One Place

Our intelligent payment tools can integrate directly with your existing EMR or practice management system. The result is fewer outstanding balances, faster payments, and an improved experience for every patient who walks through your doors.
