Pricing FAQ

Are there any setup fees?

We offer free onboarding for annual contracts. Month-to-month plans have a one-time setup fee based on your environment complexity.

What's the minimum contract?

We offer both month-to-month and annual agreements. Annual contracts receive a 10% discount on all services.

Can I change my package later?

Absolutely! You can add or remove services at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes! Organizations with 100+ employees receive tiered discounts. Contact us for custom enterprise pricing.
